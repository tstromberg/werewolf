# SSH bastion on Werewolf

A forwarding-only OpenSSH server, by security key: its users, their keys
and the destinations each may reach are written in your form and built
into the image. [The bastion's README](../../forms/bastion/README.md) is
the whole tutorial: making keys, describing users in form.yaml, creating
the machine, connecting through it, taking key files, and what it refuses.
